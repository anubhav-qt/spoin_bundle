# The feed became something I want to use: 2026-08-27

Not an ADR (no decision), not a task (no work scoped). Same reason
[the Reddit validation log](2026-08-24-first-public-validation-run.md) got written down: a
signal worth keeping that would otherwise evaporate in a chat log.

## What happened

While verifying the responsive-shell work on this branch (`responsive-mobile-shell` —
[ADR-0064](../adr/0064-mobile-shell-tab-bar.md)'s mobile shell, the right-rail restructure,
the Obsidian + Space Grotesk developer's-pick markers), I caught myself scrolling the feed
on purpose. Not clicking through to check a breakpoint or confirm a chevron shows on the
right side — actually reading a card because I wanted to, then swiping to the next one.

Had to stop and notice I'd drifted from testing into using.

## Why it's logged

The feed frontend was declared finalized this session
([apps/web/README.md](../../apps/web/README.md) status section). "Finalized" is a
checklist state — build green, breakpoints verified, screenshots taken. This is the other
kind of done: the thing stopped feeling like scaffolding I was poking at and started
feeling like a product I'd open on my own.

`n=1`, it's my own feed, I'm the least objective possible tester. Doesn't matter. The
whole pitch is "something you'd actually reach for instead of doomscrolling," and the
person who has seen every rough edge and every gate rejection reached for it instead of
the tab that was one keystroke away. That's the first time that's been true.

Future-me: this is what the frontend crossing the line looked like. It felt good.
